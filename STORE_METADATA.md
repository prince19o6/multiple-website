## 4. Multi-Brand Submission Strategy (Apple Guideline 4.3 Anti-Spam)

Apple Guideline 4.3 states: *"Don't create multiple bundle IDs of the same app. If your app has different versions for specific locations, teams, or clients, consider using a single app and provide the variations using in-app purchase, device features, or location."*

### Strategy Comparison & Recommended Path

| Strategy | Architecture | Pros | Cons / Risks | Apple Review Risk | Recommendation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: |
| **Option A: Separate Apple Developer Accounts** | Each brand has its own legal entity & D-U-N-S number on developer.apple.com | Clean separation; 100% compliant with white-label guidelines; custom marketing per brand | Requires multiple $99/year Apple accounts and business registrations | **Zero Risk** | **Best for Independent Clients** |
| **Option B: Multi-Brand Container App** | Single App on App Store (`ToursApp Travel Explorer`) with in-app brand selector or first-launch onboarding | Single $99/year account; zero risk of 4.3 rejection; shared maintenance | Less customized icon on home screen | **Zero Risk** | **Best for Single Entity** |
| **Option C: Branded Bundle IDs on One Account** | Submitting 3 separate apps (`com.toursapp.gujjutours`, `tripgoasia`, `technoheaven`) under one developer account | Individual brand icons on home screen | High risk of Apple flagging as Guideline 4.3 "Template / Duplicate App" | **High Risk** | **Not Recommended without Differentiation** |

### Recommended Action Plan:
- **If submitting under one Apple account:** Use **Option B (Multi-Brand Container App)** or heavily differentiate the feature sets per brand (e.g. Gujju Tours features domestic Indian & Dubai tours; TripGo Asia specializes strictly in Southeast Asian island expeditions; Technoheaven focuses exclusively on VIP luxury B2B travel desks).
- **If each brand is owned by a distinct legal company:** Use **Option A (Separate Developer Accounts)** using Apple's custom B2B or standard client enrollment.
