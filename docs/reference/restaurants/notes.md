# Live Site Reference Audit: Restaurants & Dining Module (`/en/restaurants`)

## 1. Landing Page (`/en/restaurants`)
- **URL**: `https://www.gujjutours.com/en/restaurants`
- **Hero Title**: "Discover the Best Dining Experiences"
- **Search Widget**:
  - "Where would you like to eat?" Destination City autocomplete (Dubai, Singapore, Bangkok, Mumbai, Goa).
  - Cuisine dropdown (Indian, Gujarati Thali, Jain, Pure Veg, Asian, Fine Dining).
  - Reservation Date & Time.
  - Party Size (Guests count).
  - CTA: "Find Restaurants".
- **Landing Sections & Collections (Pre-Search)**:
  - "Explore Amazing Restaurants" (Featured Restaurant Cards):
    - Kathiyawadi Thali Restaurant (Authentic Gujarati, Pure Veg, Jain Options).
    - Maharaja Bhog (Premium Royal Vegetarian Thali).
    - Khandani Rajdhani (Heritage Thali Dining).
    - Rangoli Restaurant (Street food, chaat, Gujarati & North Indian).
    - Atithi Veg Restaurant (South & North Indian pure veg).
  - Cuisine Categories chips with icons.
  - Dietary Preference Badges: `100% Jain Pure`, `Pure Vegetarian`, `Halal Certified`, `Vegan Friendly`.

## 2. Restaurant Card Structure
- Restaurant Hero Photo.
- Badges: `New`, `Pure Veg`, `Jain Available`, `Fine Dining`.
- Title & Location Area (e.g. `Al Karama, Dubai`).
- Cuisine types tags.
- Average Cost for Two (e.g. `₹1,200 for two`).
- Rating (`4.7 (890 reviews)`).
- CTA: "View Menu & Reserve".

## 3. Detail Page (`/en/restaurants/<slug>/<id>`)
- High-res photo gallery of dishes and ambiance.
- Dietary certification details (Guaranteed no onion, no garlic options for Jain travelers).
- Highlights & Signature Dishes list.
- Operating Hours & Seating capacity.
- Table Reservation booking form (Date, Slot, Party size, Special dietary notes) with instant confirmation voucher.
