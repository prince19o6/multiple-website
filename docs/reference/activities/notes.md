# Live Site Reference Audit: Activities & Tours Module (`/en/tours`)

## 1. Landing Page (`/en/tours`)
- **URL**: `https://www.gujjutours.com/en/tours`
- **Hero Title**: "Top Tourist Attractions & Experiences"
- **Search Widget**:
  - Fields: Destination or experience name autocomplete, Travel Date picker, Travelers count.
  - Search CTA: "Search Experiences" / "Search Tours".
- **Landing Sections & Collections (Pre-Search)**:
  - "Best Cities to Visit" (Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Singapore, Bangkok, Phuket, Bali).
  - "Top Things To Do WorldWide" (Top attractions across globe).
  - "Things to Do in Dubai" (Desert Safari, Burj Khalifa At The Top, Dubai Marina Dhow Cruise, Museum of the Future).
  - "Must-Do Activities in Singapore" (Universal Studios Singapore, Gardens by the Bay, Night Safari).
  - "Experience the 7 Wonders of the World" (Taj Mahal, Colosseum, Petra, Great Wall of China).
  - "Top Activities to Experience in Bangkok" (Grand Palace, Chao Phraya Princess Cruise, Safari World).
  - "Trending Activities" (Curated list with badges: "Bestseller", "Instant Confirmation").

## 3. Results Page (`/en/tours/<city>`)
- **Filters**:
  - Category / Travel Style chips.
  - Duration (Up to 2 hours, 2-4 hours, Full Day, Multi-Day).
  - Price Range slider.
  - Rating (4.5+ stars).
  - Features: Instant Confirmation, Free Cancellation, Mobile Voucher, Hotel Pick-up Included.
- **Activity Card Item**:
  - High-res cover image.
  - Category pill and City name.
  - Activity Title.
  - Duration and timing (e.g. `4 Hours • Starting at 15:30`).
  - Rating & Review count (e.g. `⭐ 4.9 (2,180)`).
  - Inclusions badges (`Hotel Transfers Included`, `Meal Included`).
  - Pricing (Original, discount %, effective price per person).
  - CTA: "Explore Options".

## 4. Detail Page (`/en/tours/<city>/<category>/<slug>-<id>`)
- Breadcrumbs, photo gallery & virtual walkthrough reel.
- Detailed overview & highlights bullets.
- Tour Options Matrix (Ticket Only, Ticket + Shared Transfer, Ticket + Private Luxury Transfer, VIP Fast Pass).
- Itinerary breakdown with timeline steps.
- What's Included & What's Excluded lists.
- Important Information (Dress code, age limits, baggage limits).
- Meeting point / Pick-up notes with interactive map pin.
- Cancellation Policy (e.g. `Free cancellation up to 24 hours in advance for a full refund`).
